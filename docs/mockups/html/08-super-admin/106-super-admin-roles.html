Super Admin stays out of the work. It manages accounts and reads analytics, reports and the audit log. It cannot open leads, proposals, projects or payments.
Roles are fixed. You choose which role an account has, from the user’s page. “Own only” means they see just their own items: a client sees only their own proposals and payments, a developer only the tasks assigned to them.
