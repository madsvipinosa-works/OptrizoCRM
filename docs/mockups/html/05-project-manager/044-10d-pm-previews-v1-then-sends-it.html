2. Your needs and goals

From our discovery meeting on Nov 4, 2026, we understand that:

  • Dispatchers track deliveries by phone and spreadsheets, so delays are found late.
  • Drivers have no simple way to confirm a delivery with proof.
  • Managers cannot see daily delivery performance in one place.
Goals
  • Track every delivery and driver from one mobile app and dashboard.
  • Capture proof of delivery with photos.
  • Give managers clear daily and weekly reports.

3. Proposed solution

A driver mobile app and a dispatcher web dashboard that share one database.

  • Driver app (Android and iOS): assigned deliveries, navigation link, status updates, proof photos.
  • Dispatcher dashboard: live delivery list, filters by date, driver, and status, reassignment.
  • Admin reports: on-time rate and deliveries per driver, exportable to Excel.
