International clients: no pricing meeting and no lawyer review. The PM sends the proposal with the price as v1 (step 17 directly), and the client signs (19).
Numbering: plain numbers are the main path. Letters (18a, 26a) are other paths from that step. “Ref” boards are screens a role can open at any time, such as dashboards and notifications.
Hand-offs are notifications: each time work passes to another role, that role gets a notification and a red badge, so no one has to check by hand.
